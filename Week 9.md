# Week 9: Identity and Access Management

## Learning Outcomes

Students must demonstrate technical execution, evidence collection, analysis and reporting skills.

## Objective

The objective of this practical was to investigate user accounts, groups, and permissions within Ubuntu Linux. Identity and Access Management (IAM) helps ensure users have appropriate access according to the principle of least privilege.

## Reflection

This practical focused on identity and access management within Ubuntu Linux. User account information, group memberships, file permissions, and administrative privileges were reviewed to understand how access controls are implemented and managed.

Identity and Access Management is essential in cybersecurity because it ensures that users can only access resources required for their roles. Proper management of accounts, permissions, and privileges helps reduce security risks and supports SOC monitoring activities.

## Conclusion

The objectives of this practical were successfully achieved. User account information, group memberships, file permissions, and administrative privileges were reviewed and analysed. These activities demonstrated how access control mechanisms are implemented within Linux environments.

The practical highlighted the importance of least privilege, access control, and user management in maintaining secure computing environments and supporting cybersecurity operations.
