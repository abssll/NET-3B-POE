### Observation
ICMP Echo Request and Echo Reply packets were captured while performing a connectivity test to the destination host.

### Explanation
ICMP is a network protocol used for diagnostics and troubleshooting. The ping utility sends Echo Requests, and the destination responds with Echo Replies to confirm connectivity.

### Result
Four successful responses were received, confirming network connectivity between the virtual machine and the external network.
